A number of objects (e.\+g., mega-\/blocks) are stored without any specific order on a stand (initial stand) located within the workspace of a robotic manipulator. The manipulator is an anthropomorphic arm, with a spherical wrist and a two-\/fingered gripper as end-\/effector. The objects can belong to different classes but have a known geometry (coded in the STL files). The objective of the project is to use the manipulator to pick the objects in sequence and to position them on a different stand according to a specified order (final stand). A calibrated 3D sensor is used to locate the different objects and to detect their position in the initial stand.\hypertarget{md__2home_2giuntinux_2trento__lab__home_2ros__ws_2src_2lab__myproject_2README_autotoc_md9}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{Robot}{Robot}}\label{md__2home_2giuntinux_2trento__lab__home_2ros__ws_2src_2lab__myproject_2README_autotoc_md9}
